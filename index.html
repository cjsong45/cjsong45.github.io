About Me

Hi! I'm Chanjae Song, a Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Data Science, KAIST, where I am advised by Professor Jae-Gil Lee in the Data Mining Lab. I am also a research student at the SuperIntelligence Lab, LG AI Research. I received my M.S. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from KAIST under the supervision of Professor Mun Yong Yi, and my B.S. in Industrial Management Engineering from Korea University.

My research focuses on:

  • Grounded multimodal interpretability: revealing which evidence a multimodal model actually relies on, so its outputs can be traced, checked, and corrected.
  • Robust multimodal learning: keeping multimodal models reliable when inputs are missing, noisy, or uncertain, by improving the data and supervision they learn from rather than scaling the model.
